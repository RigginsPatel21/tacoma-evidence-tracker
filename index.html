Tacoma & 4Runner Accessory Evidence Tracker

What the manufacturers actually say about which trims each accessory works with, quoted, linked and dated. Where they say nothing, we say so.

Read this first

  • This is not a "will it fit" guarantee. Each row shows the evidence status of one combination of part, vehicle, years, trim and configuration.
  • "Unverified" means the maker hasn't said either way. It is not a "does not fit" result; those are labelled "Manufacturer-confirmed: not compatible". Unverified entries are hidden at first so the maker's actual statements come first. Each one names the question that is still open.
  • Statuses come from the manufacturer's own pages: listings, fitment notes and Q&A answers. Retailer listings that disagree with the manufacturer are shown as warnings and never change a status.
  • Confirm fitment with the manufacturer before you buy or install anything. Pages change; every source shows the date it was last checked.

Not covered yet, and why

    How entries are graded

    • Manufacturer-confirmed only when the maker's current page names the exact trim (or excludes it with an explicit "only" or "will NOT work" statement).
    • Condition-dependent when the maker states a requirement for this exact trim, such as factory fog lamps. A requirement stated only for the vehicle in general, or for a different trim, is recorded as Unverified with the requirement shown.
    • Conflicting when credible sources disagree, including two pages from the same maker that imply opposite answers.
    • Unverified for everything else. That includes a maker's generic "Fits Tacoma 2024-2026" line, hedged answers ("should fit", "not tested yet"), and answers that address wiring rather than fit.
    • Owner reports and retailer fitment tables are never used to set a status.
    • Nothing is carried across vehicles or years: a Tacoma statement says nothing about a 4Runner, and an answer about a 2025 truck says nothing about 2024 or 2026, unless the maker says so.
    • A maker's statement must address fitment. Answers about wiring, switches or harness length are shown, but they never confirm fit.
    • Entries marked "Under review" carry a label that a reasonable reader could grade differently. The note explains why.

    Report a correction
